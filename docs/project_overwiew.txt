Yield Matrix is an all-in-one intelligent farming platform designed for small and marginal farmers in rural India,
particularly Karnataka. The platform combines real-time data, artificial intelligence, and local language support to
give every farmer access to expert-level agricultural guidance -- completely free of charge. Yield Matrix addresses
the most critical pain points farmers face daily: unpredictable weather, undetected crop diseases, volatile market
prices, lack of crop knowledge, and missing out on government benefits.
